About Hind Scrap

India's Global Circular Materials Platform

Not a scrap dealer. A globally positioned secondary-raw-material sourcing, trading and supply network — connecting the world's recyclable resources with India's industrial demand.

Our Story

Built on a Simple but Powerful Insight

India is the world's second-largest steel producer — yet imports significant secondary raw materials without a structured, trusted global sourcing partner. Simultaneously, India's recyclable materials don't flow efficiently to the global buyers who need them most.

Hind Scrap was founded to close both gaps — a demand-led, inspection-verified, fully documented global circular materials network with India at its centre.

"We envision a future where scrap is no longer seen as waste, but as a globally traded industrial resource — verified, graded, traceable and delivered where it creates the highest value."

Logistics & Documentation

End-to-End Trade Logistics — From Origin Yard to Your Factory

International scrap trade is complex. We manage every step so your procurement team receives verified material, not paperwork problems.

🚢

Ocean Freight & Vessel Booking

Bulk vessel bookings for large consignments and containerised shipping for smaller lots — optimising freight cost and transit time for each trade lane. FOB, CIF or DAP terms negotiated per buyer requirement.

🏗️

Port Operations & Customs

Export customs clearance at origin, import customs filing at Indian ports, DGFT import licensing liaison, and port-level compliance for hazmat materials like batteries and e-waste.

📋

Trade Documentation

Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, Bill of Lading, Material Test Reports, CoA, SGS inspection report, phytosanitary certificates — complete documentary package for every shipment.

🚛

Inland Delivery

From Indian port to your plant or warehouse — bulk truck transport, rake movement for large consignments, and last-mile delivery across all major industrial cities and SEZs.

💱

Trade Finance Support

Guidance on LC (Letter of Credit) structures, CAD (Cash Against Documents) terms, and connection to trade finance providers for buyers requiring working capital support on large consignments.

🔗

Shipment Tracking

Vessel tracking, ETA updates, port arrival notifications and discharge confirmations — keeping your procurement and inventory teams informed at every stage of the shipment lifecycle.
